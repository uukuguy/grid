# Phase 03.9.1 Plan — Full route enforcement and Action matrix

## Objective

Turn the catalog into executable route-level authorization for every non-public business route while preserving None/ApiKey behavior and expanding the Action vocabulary into coherent domains.

## Threat model

- **Mitigate:** Full-mode protected route bypass caused by missing route middleware.
- **Mitigate:** Privilege escalation caused by overly broad Action reuse.
- **Mitigate:** accidental protection of login or regression of None/ApiKey compatibility.
- **Preserve:** Owner superuser invariant and shared-core leg-agnostic Role × Action semantics.

## Tasks

### Task 1 (TDD RED): matrix and full-catalog enforcement contracts

Add failing tests for:

- complete Action parsing and Role × Action policy
- Owner succeeds for every action
- Viewer succeeds only for Read
- User succeeds only for Read/CreateSession/RunAgent
- Admin additionally manages operational domains and users
- every non-public catalog route has a runtime authorization layer in Full mode
- None and ApiKey regression paths stay green

### Task 2 (TDD GREEN): coherent Action expansion

Extend `grid-engine::auth::roles::Action` with semantic management domains surfaced by the catalog: audit, hooks, memories, providers, secrets, sandbox, scheduler, security, collaboration, knowledge graph, eval, metering, and agents. Regenerate parse and Role × Action policy without product-leg branches.

### Task 3 (TDD GREEN): catalog-driven router construction

Provide helpers that construct `MethodRouter` values with `RequiredAction` middleware from catalog metadata. Rewrite route assembly so every business method/path receives its catalog Action. Public entries remain outside RBAC. Reuse `require_action_middleware` unchanged except exhaustive legacy Permission mapping required by new enum variants.

### Task 4: targeted regressions and independent security review

Run targeted engine role tests, route catalog/enforcement tests, `test_auth_modes`, and v3.8 multi-user suites. Perform a separate review pass focused on bypasses, route ordering, login/public exposure, and privilege matrix coherence; fix confirmed findings.

## Success criteria

- Full mode executes Role × Action authorization on every non-public HTTP route.
- None and ApiKey behavior remains compatible.
- Catalog and runtime router cannot drift silently.
- All eight phase requirements have executable evidence.
